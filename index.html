Print settings

 mm = 1 km

Bigger number = bigger print, finer detail.

Recesses open ocean (sea-level, edge-connected) so it reads as water; inland basins stay land. Insert = a flat blue slab filling the recess to sea level (needs ≥1 mm drop); it prints flat and drops into the recess as-is.

Bands recolor the terrain top by altitude. Readout heights are approximate (preview frame); exact values are written at export. Embedded changes load only when the .3mf is opened as a project in PrusaSlicer (not "import geometry").

Ribbon prints flat and flexes into the groove; sharp relief along the route is embossed on its top, standing ~0.6 mm proud of the terrain.

Prints as a separate cord that sits on the terrain surface, its underside molded to the terrain so it self-locates. The terrain model is left unmodified; steep sections need slicer supports under the cord.
